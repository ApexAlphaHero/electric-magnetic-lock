This journal is volatile

/var/log/journal does not exist, so systemd is keeping the journal in RAM and everything on this page is lost on reboot — including whatever was logged by the fault that caused the reboot. To keep it: 

sudo mkdir -p /var/log/journal
sudo systemctl restart systemd-journald

That moves the journal onto the SD card, so weigh it against card wear and set SystemMaxUse= in /etc/systemd/journald.conf to bound it.
